# A Deeper China | Guilin–Yangshuo draft

**Shape:** 3 days, built around Karst landscape, not a checklist · Yulong River and slow countryside movement · Weather, water and route handoffs · Li River as a water decision.

## Why this landscape
This is not a scenery conveyor belt. It chooses one water decision, one land-based layer and room for weather and recovery.

## Suggested building blocks
- **Karst landscape, not a checklist** — What relationship to the landscape do you actually want?
- **Yulong River and slow countryside movement** — How can I experience Yangshuo without assuming a bike or raft?
- **Weather, water and route handoffs** — Can this plan survive rain, fog, water changes and intercity transfers?
- **Li River as a water decision** — Do I want a cruise, a shorter river section, or land-based views?

## On-the-ground notes
- **Landscape-intent interview:** Karst scenery serves very different visitors: a photographer, a child, a non-cyclist, a climber and someone who wants rest do not need the same Yangshuo. **Check:** vague_intent, overpacking.
- **Xingping / iconic karst-view decision:** A recognisable river-and-peak landscape choice, best when image, walking tolerance and crowd expectations are made explicit. **Check:** weather, crowds, transport, terrain.
- **Yulong River slow-movement decision:** The quieter countryside/river relationship often suits visitors who want movement at their own pace. **Check:** weather, water_level, transport, crowds.
- **Non-cycling Yangshuo branch:** Karst travel is viable without cycling or scooters; forcing unfamiliar road riding is a product failure. **Check:** mobility, transport, road_safety.
- **Three-night core decision:** For three nights, use arrival/recovery, one river/karst anchor and one slower countryside day; do not add Longji by default. **Check:** overpacking, weather, transfer_fatigue.
- **Five-night depth decision:** Only at this scale can Longji or a second landscape mode become meaningful without erasing rest and weather flexibility. **Check:** weather, fatigue, transport.
- **Li River cruise decision:** A cruise can be a major landscape day, not merely transport from Guilin to Yangshuo. **Check:** water_level, weather, motion_sickness, luggage, ticketing.

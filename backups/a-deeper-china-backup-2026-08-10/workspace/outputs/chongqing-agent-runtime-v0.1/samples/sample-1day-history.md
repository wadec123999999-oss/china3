# Chongqing Roadbook — 1-Day Plan

> Planning status: **route draft ready**. Live opening, weather and operational checks may still be required before release.

## Design frame

- Available time: 1 day
- Intended pace: moderate walking load
- Information budget: no more than 3 modules per day

Hard constraints were applied before preferences. The route deliberately favours a coherent explanation of Chongqing over a checklist of attractions.

## Day 1 — Hongya Cave is a visual entry point, not a shopping complex that must be explored from end to end.

### Reading the Night Skyline—Without Getting Lost Inside Hongya Cave

**Where:** Daijiaxiang — Hongya Cave exterior — Grand Theatre riverfront  
**Working duration:** about 150 minutes

From across the river, read bridges, water, cliffs, buildings and light as one urban cross-section.

**The one idea to carry:** Hongya Cave is a visual entry point, not a shopping complex that must be explored from end to end.

**Your observation task:** Point out the ridge, riverbank and bridges you travelled through earlier in the day.

**Reality check:** Night crowds can be heavy; lighting, drone performances and traffic controls depend on current official notices.

**Confirm before release:** Preferred end time and crowd tolerance.

### Migrant Networks and the Water Gate

**Where:** Huguang Guild Hall — Dongshuimen  
**Working duration:** about 130 minutes

Use guild halls, mutual-aid networks and a river gate to understand why Chongqing became a migrant city.

**The one idea to carry:** Guild halls supported newcomers through commerce, belief and social organisation.

**Your observation task:** Compare what the guild hall did with how the water gate connected to the terrain.

**Reality check:** Current opening and English-language support must be checked before departure.

**Confirm before release:** Desired history depth and current venue opening.

### Wartime Capital on Foot

**Where:** Zhongshan Fourth Road: Guiyuan — Zhou Enlai Residence — Te Yuan  
**Working duration:** about 120 minutes

Place politics, negotiation, diplomacy and cultural activity inside one walkable street.

**The one idea to carry:** Tell history through relationships and choices rather than a pile of names and dates.

**Your observation task:** Compare the domestic scale of the buildings with the international political work they once held.

**Reality check:** Allow about two hours; the amount of political history should match the guest’s interest.

**Confirm before release:** Desired depth of political and wartime history.

# Chongqing Roadbook — 3-Day Plan

> Planning status: **draft awaiting essential confirmation**. Do not treat this as a released route yet.

## Design frame

- Available time: 3 days
- Intended pace: lower walking load
- Information budget: no more than 3 modules per day

Hard constraints were applied before preferences. The route deliberately favours a coherent explanation of Chongqing over a checklist of attractions.

## Confirm before the route is released

- How long can everyone walk continuously, and are repeated stairs a problem?
- What time can you start on the first day, and when must you leave on the last day?

## Day 1 — Mountain terrain allows several street levels to be simultaneously true.

### The Multiple-Ground-Level Experiment

**Where:** Kuixinglou and connected high-level platforms  
**Working duration:** about 40 minutes

Test for yourself how a 22nd floor can also meet a street—the fastest way to understand Chongqing’s vertical logic.

**The one idea to carry:** Mountain terrain allows several street levels to be simultaneously true.

**Your observation task:** Guess the floor first, then leave through a different exit and test your assumption.

**Reality check:** Tell us about serious fear of heights in advance; the exercise does not require approaching an exposed edge.

**Confirm before release:** Tolerance for exposed heights.

### Reading the Night Skyline—Without Getting Lost Inside Hongya Cave

**Where:** Daijiaxiang — Hongya Cave exterior — Grand Theatre riverfront  
**Working duration:** about 150 minutes

From across the river, read bridges, water, cliffs, buildings and light as one urban cross-section.

**The one idea to carry:** Hongya Cave is a visual entry point, not a shopping complex that must be explored from end to end.

**Your observation task:** Point out the ridge, riverbank and bridges you travelled through earlier in the day.

**Reality check:** Night crowds can be heavy; lighting, drone performances and traffic controls depend on current official notices.

**Confirm before release:** Preferred end time and crowd tolerance.

### Gateway Between Two Rivers

**Where:** Chaotianmen and the confluence viewpoint  
**Working duration:** about 75 minutes

Use the former city gate and the directions of the two rivers to understand why Chongqing grew here.

**The one idea to carry:** Chaotianmen was a gate and river port before it became a modern skyline.

**Your observation task:** Reconstruct the directions of the Yangtze, Jialing and the Yuzhong peninsula from the riverfront.

**Reality check:** Riverfront entrances and walking distance must be adjusted to the access available on the day.

**Confirm before release:** Walking tolerance and current riverfront access.


## Day 2 — Chongqing’s strangeness is not just verticality; it is the coexistence of different urban orders.

### Orienting Chongqing: Civic Landmark and Sacred Enclave

**Where:** Jiefangbei — Luohan Temple  
**Working duration:** about 75 minutes

Establish your bearings, then feel how a working Buddhist temple occupies a pocket between towers.

**The one idea to carry:** Chongqing’s strangeness is not just verticality; it is the coexistence of different urban orders.

**Your observation task:** Compare sound, light and walking pace on the two sides of the temple gate.

**Reality check:** If time is short, this can be folded into the following vertical-city module.

### Migrant Networks and the Water Gate

**Where:** Huguang Guild Hall — Dongshuimen  
**Working duration:** about 130 minutes

Use guild halls, mutual-aid networks and a river gate to understand why Chongqing became a migrant city.

**The one idea to carry:** Guild halls supported newcomers through commerce, belief and social organisation.

**Your observation task:** Compare what the guild hall did with how the water gate connected to the terrain.

**Reality check:** Current opening and English-language support must be checked before departure.

**Confirm before release:** Desired history depth and current venue opening.

### Noodles, Hotpot and the Social Rules of Eating

**Where:** Chongqing noodles — bilingual hotpot experience  
**Working duration:** about 180 minutes

Enter everyday life through breakfast, then use hotpot to understand a shared table, spice levels and social rules.

**The one idea to carry:** A restaurant list is not the product; ordering, allergies, spice and shared-table rules are.

**Your observation task:** Complete one order with explanation instead of having every decision made for you.

**Reality check:** Allergens, dietary restrictions and spice tolerance must be confirmed; price and cancellation terms are separate.

**Confirm before release:** Allergens, dietary restrictions, spice tolerance and budget.


## Day 3 — The Liziba viewpoint is a verification point; the ride is the core experience.

### Ride First, Photograph Second

**Where:** Metro Line 2 — Liziba  
**Working duration:** about 70 minutes

Understand how the monorail responds to curves, slope and dense construction before treating the building passage as a photograph.

**The one idea to carry:** The Liziba viewpoint is a verification point; the ride is the core experience.

**Your observation task:** From the train, record elevated track, river edge, curves and changing ground levels.

**Reality check:** Construction or crowds may alter the exterior stop; the train ride remains the core.

**Confirm before release:** Motion sensitivity and photography purpose.

### A Museum Framework for the City

**Where:** Three Gorges Museum — Great Hall exterior  
**Working duration:** about 180 minutes

Use terrain, migration, the Three Gorges and wartime history to explain what you saw on the street.

**The one idea to carry:** Visit only the galleries that answer the route’s questions rather than trying to complete the museum.

**Your observation task:** Name three city phenomena that make more sense when you leave.

**Reality check:** Monday closure patterns and temporary exhibition changes can affect access; check again before departure.

**Confirm before release:** Current opening and children’s ages.

### Wartime Capital on Foot

**Where:** Zhongshan Fourth Road: Guiyuan — Zhou Enlai Residence — Te Yuan  
**Working duration:** about 120 minutes

Place politics, negotiation, diplomacy and cultural activity inside one walkable street.

**The one idea to carry:** Tell history through relationships and choices rather than a pile of names and dates.

**Your observation task:** Compare the domestic scale of the buildings with the international political work they once held.

**Reality check:** Allow about two hours; the amount of political history should match the guest’s interest.

**Confirm before release:** Desired depth of political and wartime history.

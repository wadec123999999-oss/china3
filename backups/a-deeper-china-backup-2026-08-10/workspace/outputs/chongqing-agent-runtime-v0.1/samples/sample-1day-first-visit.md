# Chongqing Roadbook — 1-Day Plan

> Planning status: **route draft ready**. Live opening, weather and operational checks may still be required before release.

## Design frame

- Available time: 1 day
- Intended pace: moderate walking load
- Information budget: no more than 3 modules per day

Hard constraints were applied before preferences. The route deliberately favours a coherent explanation of Chongqing over a checklist of attractions.

## Day 1 — Guild halls supported newcomers through commerce, belief and social organisation.

### Migrant Networks and the Water Gate

**Where:** Huguang Guild Hall — Dongshuimen  
**Working duration:** about 130 minutes

Use guild halls, mutual-aid networks and a river gate to understand why Chongqing became a migrant city.

**The one idea to carry:** Guild halls supported newcomers through commerce, belief and social organisation.

**Your observation task:** Compare what the guild hall did with how the water gate connected to the terrain.

**Reality check:** Current opening and English-language support must be checked before departure.

**Confirm before release:** Desired history depth and current venue opening.

### Noodles, Hotpot and the Social Rules of Eating

**Where:** Chongqing noodles — bilingual hotpot experience  
**Working duration:** about 180 minutes

Enter everyday life through breakfast, then use hotpot to understand a shared table, spice levels and social rules.

**The one idea to carry:** A restaurant list is not the product; ordering, allergies, spice and shared-table rules are.

**Your observation task:** Complete one order with explanation instead of having every decision made for you.

**Reality check:** Allergens, dietary restrictions and spice tolerance must be confirmed; price and cancellation terms are separate.

**Confirm before release:** Allergens, dietary restrictions, spice tolerance and budget.

### Ride First, Photograph Second

**Where:** Metro Line 2 — Liziba  
**Working duration:** about 70 minutes

Understand how the monorail responds to curves, slope and dense construction before treating the building passage as a photograph.

**The one idea to carry:** The Liziba viewpoint is a verification point; the ride is the core experience.

**Your observation task:** From the train, record elevated track, river edge, curves and changing ground levels.

**Reality check:** Construction or crowds may alter the exterior stop; the train ride remains the core.

**Confirm before release:** Motion sensitivity and photography purpose.

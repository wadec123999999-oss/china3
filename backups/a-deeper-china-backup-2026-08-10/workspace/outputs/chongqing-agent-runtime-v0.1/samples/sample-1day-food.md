# Chongqing Roadbook — 1-Day Plan

> Planning status: **route draft ready**. Live opening, weather and operational checks may still be required before release.

## Design frame

- Available time: 1 day
- Intended pace: moderate walking load
- Information budget: no more than 2 modules per day

Hard constraints were applied before preferences. The route deliberately favours a coherent explanation of Chongqing over a checklist of attractions.

## Day 1 — A restaurant list is not the product; ordering, allergies, spice and shared-table rules are.

### Noodles, Hotpot and the Social Rules of Eating

**Where:** Chongqing noodles — bilingual hotpot experience  
**Working duration:** about 180 minutes

Enter everyday life through breakfast, then use hotpot to understand a shared table, spice levels and social rules.

**The one idea to carry:** A restaurant list is not the product; ordering, allergies, spice and shared-table rules are.

**Your observation task:** Complete one order with explanation instead of having every decision made for you.

**Reality check:** Allergens, dietary restrictions and spice tolerance must be confirmed; price and cancellation terms are separate.

**Confirm before release:** Allergens, dietary restrictions, spice tolerance and budget.

### Land Gate and Mountain-City Paths

**Where:** Tongyuanmen — Third Mountain City Trail — Shibati comparison  
**Working duration:** about 210 minutes

Use your feet to understand how slope, lanes, rest platforms and daily life form part of the transport system.

**The one idea to carry:** A mountain-city path is transport and social space, not merely a scenic boardwalk.

**Your observation task:** Record how a road becomes steps, a platform, a lane and the edge of a courtyard.

**Reality check:** This module contains repeated stairs; rain, knee pain or low walking capacity triggers a lower-step alternative.

**Confirm before release:** Continuous stair capacity and knee condition.

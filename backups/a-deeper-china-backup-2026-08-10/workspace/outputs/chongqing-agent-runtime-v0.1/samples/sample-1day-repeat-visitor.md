# Chongqing Roadbook — 1-Day Plan

> Planning status: **route draft ready**. Live opening, weather and operational checks may still be required before release.

## Design frame

- Available time: 1 day
- Intended pace: moderate walking load
- Information budget: no more than 3 modules per day

Hard constraints were applied before preferences. The route deliberately favours a coherent explanation of Chongqing over a checklist of attractions.

## Day 1 — Mountain terrain allows several street levels to be simultaneously true.

### The Multiple-Ground-Level Experiment

**Where:** Kuixinglou and connected high-level platforms  
**Working duration:** about 40 minutes

Test for yourself how a 22nd floor can also meet a street—the fastest way to understand Chongqing’s vertical logic.

**The one idea to carry:** Mountain terrain allows several street levels to be simultaneously true.

**Your observation task:** Guess the floor first, then leave through a different exit and test your assumption.

**Reality check:** Tell us about serious fear of heights in advance; the exercise does not require approaching an exposed edge.

**Confirm before release:** Tolerance for exposed heights.

### Art School, Murals and the Community Surface

**Where:** Huangjueping graffiti street — Sichuan Fine Arts Institute area  
**Working duration:** about 120 minutes

Distinguish academy art, community life, commissioned murals and commercial imitation.

**The one idea to carry:** The point is not to photograph every wall, but to see how art enters a neighbourhood.

**Your observation task:** Choose one street section and identify four different kinds of image-maker or producer.

**Reality check:** Campus and exhibition access may change; the public-neighbourhood observation still works without entry.

**Confirm before release:** Photography purpose and current exhibition access.

### The Teahouse as Social Space

**Where:** Jiaotong Teahouse  
**Working duration:** about 75 minutes

Enter a functioning social space without turning the visit into intrusive photography.

**The one idea to carry:** The teahouse’s value comes from its rules, regulars and ways of sharing space.

**Your observation task:** Drink tea and observe first; photograph only after venue and individual consent.

**Reality check:** Venue rules and regulars’ boundaries apply; filming, group size and price require advance agreement.

**Confirm before release:** Filming permission, group size, price and social boundaries.

# A Deeper China | Shenzhen, two-day technology observation (human-check draft)

**For:** A visitor interested in hardware, urban systems and contemporary China—not in a fictional factory tour.

## The idea

Shenzhen is most useful when you ask a precise question: how does hardware move from components and repair culture into retail, urban infrastructure and a fast-growing bay city?

## Day 1 — Hardware as a visible ecosystem

Use Huaqiangbei as an observation walk. Notice categories, repair, components, retail density and the movement from small parts to consumer products. Treat any purchase as your own independent decision: compare, verify compatibility and do not assume warranty, authenticity or exportability.

Do not photograph people, screens, products or stalls without permission. Do not ask for supplier introductions as part of a travel route.

## Day 2 — Technology becomes a city

Choose one public innovation-city layer: a currently verified public showroom/retail interface, a design programme, or the public realm around Shenzhen Bay and Nanshan. Add Nantou only when a layered old-city/urban-change question matters to you.

This day is about public evidence of technology in city life, not access to a corporate campus.

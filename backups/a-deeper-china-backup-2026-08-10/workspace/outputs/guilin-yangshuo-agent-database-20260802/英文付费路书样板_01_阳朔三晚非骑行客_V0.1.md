# A Deeper China | Guilin–Yangshuo, three nights without cycling (human-check draft)

**For:** A visitor whose priority is karst landscape and a slower countryside rhythm, but who does not want to cycle or ride a scooter.

## The idea

You do not need a bicycle to deserve Yangshuo. The route is built around one water/river decision, one land-based public landscape layer and room for weather.

## Day 1 — Arrive and recover

Treat Guilin or Yangshuo arrival as a movement day. Confirm data, payment, baggage, weather and the next day's operational status before committing to a river plan.

## Day 2 — Choose the river

Choose a Li River cruise/section only if motion tolerance, water/weather, baggage and time are suitable. Otherwise choose a verified land-based karst view.

The river is a landscape day; do not add a second major activity automatically.

## Day 3 — Yulong / countryside at your pace

Use a current verified non-cycling branch: a short public walk, vehicle-assisted access or another low-exertion landscape option. A bamboo raft is optional only after same-day weather, water and boarding conditions are checked.

## Do not add Longji

With three nights, Longji rice terraces would remove the weather buffer and turn the trip into transfers. Add it only with a separate extra day and terrain audit.

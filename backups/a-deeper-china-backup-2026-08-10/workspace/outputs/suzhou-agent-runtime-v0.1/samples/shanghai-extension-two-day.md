# A Deeper China | Suzhou draft

**Shape:** 2 days, built around Garden as an instrument of attention · Canal city and ordinary movement · Shanghai–Suzhou–Hangzhou Jiangnan choice.

## Why this Suzhou
This is not a garden checklist. It uses one garden, one city-water layer and one meaningful contrast.

## Suggested building blocks
- **Garden as an instrument of attention** — Which garden suits the way you want to look, move and rest?
- **Canal city and ordinary movement** — How do I connect gardens to the city's water and streets?
- **Shanghai–Suzhou–Hangzhou Jiangnan choice** — Which Jiangnan city earns the available days?

## On-the-ground notes
- **Humble Administrator's Garden decision:** A major garden anchor when the visitor wants layered water, pavilions and a first encounter with Suzhou garden composition. **Check:** reservation, crowds, weather.
- **Lingering Garden decision:** A different garden lens for visitors interested in sequence, interiors, rockery and framed movement. **Check:** ticketing, crowds, walking.
- **Pingjiang Road public-street decision:** A legible old-city water/street layer, useful when framed as public movement and commerce rather than timeless authenticity. **Check:** crowds, retail_noise, weather.
- **Shantang decision:** A canal-side commercial/night layer that can fit lively visitors but is not automatically the best old-city experience. **Check:** crowds, commercialization, late_return.
- **Shanghai day-trip decision:** A same-day Suzhou route should be one garden plus canal/old-city movement, not museum, Tiger Hill and water town together. **Check:** rail, transfer, overpacking.
- **Suzhou overnight decision:** Overnight is valuable for a second garden, a calm evening water layer or a deliberate water-town decision. **Check:** hotel_area, weather, arrival_time.

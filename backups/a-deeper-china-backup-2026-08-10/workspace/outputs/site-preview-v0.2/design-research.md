# A Deeper China — V0.2 design research

## Decision

The homepage should sell a decision and a deliverable, not an AI system. The visitor already has access to ChatGPT, DeepSeek and search; the differentiator is a route that has been edited around real constraints, with uncertainty made visible and a human review option.

## Positioning hierarchy

1. **Pain:** “Is my China itinerary actually doable?”
2. **Proof:** one concrete before/after itinerary edit.
3. **Product:** Reality Check at US$39, then a Deep City Roadbook from US$99.
4. **Trust:** human/local review is scoped, dated and never presented as a guarantee.
5. **Boundary:** no bookings, no ticket sales, no tour operation.

## Visual thesis

Use the visual grammar of a small, confident travel publication: warm paper, black ink, one vermilion mark, documentary photography and ruled sections. The page should feel like a route dossier someone has spent time editing—not a SaaS dashboard or a marketplace.

## What changed from V0.1

- Removed the large route “dashboard” as the hero focal point.
- Reduced equal-weight cards and decorative data.
- Made the real customer decision the main proof object.
- Put price, delivery window and service boundary next to the first CTA.
- Reduced launch-city emphasis to four core cities; the wider portfolio is secondary.
- Added a small intake form that asks for a route, not a sales lead profile.

## Conversion logic

The first action is a free route signal. It should earn the right to offer the US$39 Reality Check. The paid offer is deliberately narrow: a short audit with clear cuts, reordering, fatigue and transfer risks. Only after that should the visitor see the larger roadbook product.

## Content rule

Every place description must answer: “What decision does this place help the traveler make?” Avoid “top things to do,” generic adjectives and unreviewed operational promises.

## Production notes

Replace the temporary Unsplash hero with owned/licensed China photography. Keep the layout, typography, content hierarchy and conversion copy stable while testing different photography.

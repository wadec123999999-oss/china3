Before / generated itinerary

14 days · 6 cities · every famous thing

  • Beijing, Xi’an, Chengdu, Chongqing, Zhangjiajie, Shanghai
  • Three major transfers inside four days
  • No arrival-day recovery or rain branch
  • “Take a day trip” used as a universal answer
After / route decision

14 days · 4 cities · two protected slow days

  • Remove Zhangjiajie; save a full transfer and early start
  • Pair Chengdu and Chongqing around a real contrast
  • Put Shanghai at the end for a lower-friction exit
  • Keep one indoor anchor and one low-energy version per day
The value is not the prose. It is knowing what to delete before you book.

# A Deeper China | Jingdezhen, two days (human-check draft)

**For:** A visitor interested in porcelain, material culture, industrial history and contemporary design.

## The idea

Jingdezhen is not a ceramic-shopping stop. Use two days to understand a living system: material, process, kiln, labour, imperial standards and contemporary artistic ecology.

## Day 1 — How porcelain became a system

Use one currently verified heritage/public interpretation layer to ask how materials, technique and management produced porcelain at scale. This is not a generic dynastic history tour.

## Day 2 — Contemporary ecology, not private access

Use a current public art/design/market layer to observe how ceramics circulate today. Browse if you like, but do not treat market prices or artist claims as authentication.

Private studio visits and making sessions are not included unless a named provider has passed a separate safety, language, consent and delivery release.

# A Deeper China | Hangzhou from Shanghai, two days (human-check draft)

**For:** A visitor who wants a meaningful Jiangnan contrast rather than a rushed same-day checklist.

## The idea

Use Hangzhou to change the pace. One day gives you one carefully chosen layer; an overnight gives lake timing, tea or sacred landscape room to breathe.

## Day 1 — The lake as a way of looking

Choose a bounded West Lake section according to the weather, walking tolerance and your interest: water and mountain composition, a slow public walk, garden detail or early/late light.

Do not try to complete the lake. Preserve a seated meal and weather exit.

## Day 2 — Choose tea or sacred mountain

Choose **one**:

- Longjing landscape / tea learning, if you want to understand tea beyond a purchase;
- Lingyin and Feilai Feng, if Buddhist landscape, sculpture and mountain walking genuinely matter;
- a water-city or wetland alternative, if weather or crowds make the first two a poor fit.

Tea purchasing is always your own decision. This roadbook cannot authenticate tea, seller claims or price.

## Same-day rule

If you must return to Shanghai the same day, choose only one anchor: West Lake **or** tea **or** Lingyin. Train movement, hotel location, weather and fatigue are part of the itinerary.

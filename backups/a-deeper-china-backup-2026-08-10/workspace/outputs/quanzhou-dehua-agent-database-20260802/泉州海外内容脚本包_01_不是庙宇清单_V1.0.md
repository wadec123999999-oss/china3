# 泉州海外内容脚本包 01｜Quanzhou Is Not a Temple Checklist

## 内容目标

让海外旅行者理解泉州的真正入口：它不是“很少人知道的古城”或“很多寺庙的地方”，而是一座曾连接生产、港口、不同信仰与日常生活的海贸城市。

联合国教科文组织将“泉州：宋元中国的世界海洋商贸中心”列为世界遗产；可作为公开的历史框架，但动态开放、礼仪与摄影规则始终以当日官方/现场信息为准。[UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1561/)

## 内容边界

- 不把任何宗教场所写成“必拍景点”或神秘体验；
- 不拍礼拜、仪式、可识别的信众、儿童或私密空间；
- 不承诺进入、讲解、祭祀参与、餐饮/体验资源或拍摄许可；
- 不把“多元宗教”简化成猎奇式标签；
- 德化只作为“另一个需要额外一天的陶瓷问题”，不塞入泉州两日线。

## B. Short 02｜为什么两天不能加德化（30 秒）

**Hook**：`If you only have two days in Quanzhou, do not add Dehua just because you like ceramics.`

**旁白**：

`Quanzhou and Dehua can belong in the same deeper China conversation. But they answer different questions. Quanzhou is a maritime-city system: port history, urban movement, faith, and street life. Dehua is a separate ceramics and production question. If you add it without another day, you lose both the city and the material story. Protect the question before you protect the checklist.`

**CTA**：`Choose your China question first.`

## C. Short 03｜如何尊重地拍摄宗教城市（30 秒）

**Hook**：`The most respectful photo rule in Quanzhou is simple: do not assume.`

**旁白**：

`A sacred building can be historically important and still be a place where people practice, grieve, celebrate, or simply want privacy. Public access is not automatic permission to photograph. Follow the current on-site rule. Ask before photographing people. And let a place remain meaningful even if you leave without the image you planned.`

**CTA**：`Travel more closely. Not more invasively.`

### 逐段旁白

**0:00–0:45 — 反转**

`Quanzhou is sometimes introduced as a city of temples. That is true, but it is not enough. A list of religious sites can tell you what stands in a city. It cannot tell you how a world port worked, or why different communities left traces in the same streets.`

**0:45–2:05 — 先用“系统”理解城市**

`Try one question instead: how did production, water-land movement, institutions, belief, and daily exchange support maritime trade? Once you ask that, Quanzhou stops being a collection of old buildings. It becomes a city whose urban structure was shaped by connection.`

**2:05–3:35 — 信仰不是“多元打卡”**

`Religious spaces are part of that story, but they are not proof that a visitor is entitled to enter, photograph, or interpret everything. Some places are active, some are quiet, and rules can change. A respectful route treats faith as lived context, not content inventory.`

**3:35–5:00 — 两天的取舍**

`With two days, choose compatible public layers: a city-and-sacred-language thread, then an infrastructure-and-public-life thread. Leave Dehua for a separate third day if ceramics are the real reason you came. The point is not to maximize coverage. It is to make the connections legible.`

**5:00–6:15 — 为什么慢下来是功能，而不是美学**

`Going slowly lets you respond to access, weather, ceremony, crowds, and your own attention. It also makes space for ordinary street life, which is often where a city’s history becomes most visible. This does not mean wandering without thought. It means carrying one good question rather than ten weak ones.`

**6:15–7:20 — CTA**

`A Deeper China creates independent-travel roadbooks around questions like this: what you want to understand, what you should leave out, and what needs checking before you go. You make all reservations directly. Start a Quanzhou trip brief if a port city, not a checklist, is what you are looking for.`

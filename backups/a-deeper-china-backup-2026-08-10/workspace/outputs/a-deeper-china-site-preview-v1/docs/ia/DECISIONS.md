# IA decisions

- The previous single-page prototype was useful for testing the brand mood, but
  it mixed discovery, explanation, archive and purchase in one scroll.
- The commercial preview now separates those jobs into routes so a visitor can
  predict where a city page, a paid roadbook and the intake live.
- The homepage keeps one primary action: enter the city archive or start a
  route. It does not attempt to show every destination or every database field.
- City images are local preview assets with visible source credit on detail
  pages. Before public launch, replace or confirm each asset's license and
  attribution record.

# Navigation rules

## IA thesis

Organize by the visitor's next decision: discover a point of view, choose a
city question, understand the delivery, then start a route brief. The homepage
is an entry point, not the entire archive.

## Global navigation

- `Cities` → `/cities`: browse the archive and city questions.
- `How it reads` → `/how-it-reads`: understand the editing method.
- `Roadbooks` → `/roadbooks`: compare paid deliverables and see a sample.
- `Start with a route` → `/start`: submit a route idea.

## Local navigation

- City pages link to related city readings and the route intake.
- Roadbooks use in-page anchors for the two paid formats.
- The home page uses featured city cards but does not duplicate full city content.

## Labels

Use plain user language: `Cities`, `Roadbooks`, `Start with a route`. Avoid
internal labels such as `RAG`, `database`, `agent`, `marketplace` and `booking`.

# A Deeper China | Suzhou from Shanghai, two days (human-check draft)

**For:** A visitor who wants to understand gardens and canal-city life, not collect every Jiangnan image.

## Day 1 — One garden, then the city around it

Choose one primary garden according to the way you want to look: water and layered space, interior sequence and rockery, or a smaller-scale garden reading. Do not add a second major garden by default.

Then use a public canal/old-city walk to connect the garden to the city. Keep public/private boundaries clear.

## Day 2 — Choose a contrast

Choose **one**:

- Suzhou Museum, if architecture/collection context changes the garden reading;
- Tiger Hill, if you want hill, pagoda and broader landscape scale;
- a currently verified music/craft layer, if programme and language suit;
- Tongli or Zhouzhuang only if this is an extra, separate day and water-town atmosphere is the real goal.

## Same-day rule

For a Shanghai day trip: one garden plus one canal-city layer. Do not add Tiger Hill, a museum and a water town.

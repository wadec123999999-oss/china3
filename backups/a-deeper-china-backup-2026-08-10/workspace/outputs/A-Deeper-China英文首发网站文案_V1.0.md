## Home / Why this is different

**Section title**

`A better answer than a list.`

**Card 1 — Read the trip behind the question**

`“I want the real Shanghai” can mean architecture, food, creative neighborhoods, a calmer pace, or simply not wasting a short trip. We look for the decision underneath the request.`

**Card 2 — Make trade-offs visible**

`A strong route is not the one with the most stops. It makes room for weather, queues, tired feet, changing plans, and the things that cannot be reserved by confidence alone.`

**Card 3 — Check what changes**

`Opening arrangements, access, pace, and practical friction can change. A Human-checked roadbook records what was reviewed, when, and what you still need to confirm yourself.`

## Home / How it works

**Title**

`From a conversation to a route you can use.`

1. **Tell us what matters.** `A city, a mood, a question, or a trip already half-planned is enough to begin.`
2. **Get a considered first direction.** `Our database helps the conversation find the right city, pace, and cultural thread.`
3. **Choose a human check when you need one.** `For a specific trip, we review the details that are time-sensitive or easy to get wrong.`
4. **Travel independently.** `Your roadbook gives you a main line, a fallback, and the decisions to make directly.`

## How it works / what “human-checked” means

**Headline**

`Checked is not the same as guaranteed.`

**Body**

`A Human-checked roadbook records the date on which relevant details were reviewed and makes uncertainty visible. It does not promise clear skies, available tickets, open routes, private access, or an outcome outside anyone’s control.`

**Three bullets**

- `We check the relevant moving parts for your trip.`
- `We give you a main plan and an honest fallback.`
- `You remain in control of reservations, payments, and on-the-ground choices.`

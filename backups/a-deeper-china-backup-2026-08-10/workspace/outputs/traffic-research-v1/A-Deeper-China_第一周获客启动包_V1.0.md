## 三、3个路线前后对比案例

以下案例是内容示例，不是现场核验记录。对外发布时应标注：`Illustrative route review — not field-verified.`

### 案例1：14天六城路线

**Before**

```text
Beijing → Xi’an → Chengdu → Chongqing → Zhangjiajie → Shanghai
14 days, first China trip, wants everything famous.
```

**What breaks**

- 三次长距离转场集中在中段；
- 没有抵达日恢复时间；
- 张家界会把路线拉出城市文化主线；
- 每个城市都只能得到“到此一游”的时间。

**After**

```text
Beijing → Chengdu → Chongqing → Shanghai
14 days, two protected slow days, Zhangjiajie removed.
```

**Decision**

```text
The value is not adding another destination.
It is knowing which destination to remove before you book.
```

### 案例2：上海—成都—重庆

**Before**

```text
12 nights, architecture and food, wants to see as much as possible.
Shanghai 3 nights → Chengdu 3 nights → Chongqing 2 nights.
```

**What breaks**

- 上海第一天可能被抵达和入住消耗；
- 重庆只安排两晚，却把武隆作为默认日游；
- 没有给成都留出无计划的下午。

**After**

```text
Shanghai 4 nights → Chengdu 4 nights → Chongqing 4 nights.
Protect one slow afternoon in each city.
Make Wulong optional, not automatic.
```

**Decision**

```text
Chengdu and Chongqing are useful together because of their contrast.
They should not be planned as the same kind of city.
```

### 案例3：亲子10天

**Before**

```text
Two adults and one child, 10 days, Beijing → Shanghai → Chengdu → Guilin.
The family wants culture, pandas, food and scenery.
```

**What breaks**

- 四个城市对10天亲子旅行过于分散；
- 每次转场都叠加行李、入住和孩子恢复时间；
- 桂林的加入没有足够缓冲；
- 没有雨天和低能量版本。

**After**

```text
Beijing → Shanghai → Chengdu
Add one flexible day and one indoor fallback per city.
```

**Decision**

```text
For a family route, fewer transfers are part of the experience — not wasted time.
```

## 四、3条短视频：直接拍

### 视频1：删掉一个城市

**标题：** `I would remove one city from this 14-day China itinerary.`

**英文口播：**

```text
You have 14 days in China and want Beijing, Shanghai, Chengdu,
Chongqing and Zhangjiajie.

That route is possible on paper, but I would remove one stop.
The hidden cost is not only the train ticket. It is hotel check-in,
local transport and recovery time around every transfer.

Start by asking which city you are least excited about.
That is usually the one to cut first.

Comment your route and I’ll tell you what I would rethink first.
```

**画面：**你本人开头5秒 → 路线文字 → 地图箭头 → 结尾出现Keep / Cut / Reorder。

### 视频2：成都和重庆

**标题：** `Chengdu and Chongqing are not the same trip.`

**英文口播：**

```text
Chengdu and Chongqing work well together, but not because they are similar.

Chengdu needs room for food, tea and an unhurried afternoon.
Chongqing needs more attention to hills, distance and the time it takes
to move between levels of the city.

Plan them as a contrast, not as two more boxes to tick.
```

**画面：**成都慢节奏素材 → 重庆坡道/夜景素材 → 一张对比字幕。

### 视频3：亲子路线

**标题：** `How many cities can a family really handle in 10 days in China?`

**英文口播：**

```text
For a family trip, the question is not how many cities are possible.
It is how many arrival days, hotel changes and long transfers your child
can actually enjoy.

In ten days, I would usually protect fewer cities and add a rainy-day
and low-energy version for every stop.

Fewer transfers are not wasted time. They are part of the trip.
```

**画面：**行李/车站/休息画面 → 3城路线 → 雨天备用图标。

## 五、5条Reddit回答草稿

发布前必须根据原帖的国家、月份、旅行者年龄和兴趣改写。不要完全复制。

### 回答1：14天四城

```text
Short answer: four cities in 14 days is possible, but I would protect at
least two slower days and avoid adding a fifth long-transfer stop.

The real issue is not only train speed. Arrival, hotel check-in, local
transport and recovery time all belong to the travel day.

I would decide what to remove based on the city you are least excited about,
not the place that is most famous.
```

### 回答2：成都和重庆是否一起去

```text
They can work very well together because the contrast is useful.

Chengdu needs room for food, tea and a slower afternoon. Chongqing is more
about topography, movement, river crossings and changing levels of the city.

Do not give them identical day plans. Treat them as two different readings
of the region.
```

### 回答3：第一次来中国选北京还是上海

```text
It depends on what you want your first impression to answer.

Choose Beijing if you want history, scale and major imperial sites.
Choose Shanghai if you want architecture, neighborhoods, river light and
an easier introduction to a highly connected modern city.

If you have enough time, they are complementary. If you have only a few days,
choose the city that matches your strongest interest rather than trying to
sample both quickly.
```

### 回答4：要不要加张家界

```text
I would not add Zhangjiajie automatically to every first China route.

It can be worth it if the landscape is the main reason for your trip, but it
adds a long transfer and a very different rhythm. For a city, food and culture
focused trip, I would first protect the cities already on your list.
```

### 回答5：亲子旅行

```text
For a family, count transfer days as real travel days.

The train ride may be short, but packing, reaching the station, finding the
hotel, eating and recovering all take time. I would rather give a child fewer
cities with one indoor fallback and one slower afternoon than add another famous
place and turn the trip into constant movement.
```

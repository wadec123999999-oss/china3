# 重庆海外内容脚本包 01｜Cyberpunk Chongqing Is Not a Viewpoint

## 内容目标

吸引被“cyberpunk Chongqing”视觉内容打动的海外旅行者，同时把兴趣从“拍同一张图”引向更有价值的城市判断：**这座城的视觉强度来自地形、交通、河流和多层行走系统如何共同工作。**

## 不能越过的边界

- 不承诺特定平台、交通、观景点、夜景、天气、桥梁通行或施工状态；
- 不提供无人机起飞点、空域判断、商业拍摄许可或“网红机位”；
- 不拍居民近脸、私宅、工作人员工作过程或受限区域；
- 不把轨道车厢当拍摄场地，不妨碍乘客和站务；
- 所有交通、施工、拍摄与天气信息在出行/拍摄日另行官方核验。

## B. Short 02｜重庆、大足、武隆不是一件事（35 秒）

**Hook**：`Chongqing, Dazu, and Wulong are not three stops on the same day.`

**旁白**：

`People often add them because they share a map label. But they answer three different travel questions. Chongqing is about vertical urban life, movement, food, and modern intensity. Dazu is about stone-carved religious art and historical interpretation. Wulong is a landscape and outdoor-conditions decision. Choose the question first. Then protect the travel day. A deeper China trip is not the one that collects every name.`

**画面**：重庆主城公共空间与交通 → 抽象的石刻纹理（必须自有或获授权）→ 山地/自然细节（不得假装当天实拍/不得承诺天气）→ 三项问题卡。

**CTA**：`Choose the China question before you choose the route.`

## C. Short 03｜雾天仍然能拍（30 秒）

**Hook**：`If Chongqing is foggy, stop chasing the skyline.`

**旁白**：

`Fog is not a failed Chongqing day. It just changes the frame. Move from panorama to infrastructure: stairs, retaining walls, rails, bridge layers, river-level movement, and the way a dense city meets a steep slope. Do not promise the shot you saw online. Build a visual story that can survive the weather you actually get.`

**CTA**：`Plan for the city you get, not the one in the thumbnail.`

## D. 7–9 分钟 YouTube 长视频

### 标题候选

1. `Cyberpunk Chongqing: The City Behind the Image`
2. `Chongqing Is Not a Viewpoint: How to Read China’s Vertical City`
3. `Why Chongqing Looks Unreal (and How to Plan It Without Chasing Photos)`

### 分段旁白

**0:00–0:45 — 反转**

`Chongqing has become one of the most photographed cities in China. A train seems to pass through a building. Roads appear above roads. Bridges cut across humid night air. But a city is not deep because it looks unreal. It becomes interesting when you understand what the image is showing you.`

**0:45–2:00 — 不是滤镜，是地形和系统**

`Chongqing’s visual intensity comes from terrain, rivers, dense development, and transport systems negotiating all of that at once. The city asks you to think vertically. A route that only jumps from viewpoint to viewpoint will give you photographs, but not the city’s logic.`

**2:00–3:30 — 先选择一个城市问题**

`For a first visit, choose one: do you want to understand the rivers and the city’s scale? Do you want to follow transport and vertical circulation? Or do you want a food-and-neighborhood route that explains how people live in a high-intensity landscape? The answer decides what to leave out.`

**3:30–5:10 — 赛博感如何变成路线**

`A strong day moves between a big-scale observation and a human-scale movement sequence. You notice a bridge or river geometry, then ride or walk a section that reveals the constraint behind the image. The route should include an exit: heat, crowds, rain, and steepness all change what remains enjoyable.`

**5:10–6:25 — 不要误把社媒画面当可复制承诺**

`A post cannot tell you if there was a crowd-control barrier, construction, weather, a camera restriction, or a private rooftop involved. It cannot give you permission to fly a drone or film people. Treat online images as visual references, not operating instructions.`

**6:25–7:40 — 重庆、大足、武隆的取舍**

`The common planning mistake is putting Chongqing, Dazu, and Wulong into one mental bucket. They are not interchangeable. They demand different time, energy, transport, and interests. If urban intensity is why you came, give Chongqing the time it needs. If stone-carved Buddhist art or landscapes are the real reason, say that early and redesign the trip around it.`

**7:40–8:30 — CTA**

`A Deeper China does not sell the perfect shot. It helps you choose the question, protect the route, and know what needs checking before you go. Start a Chongqing trip brief with your dates, energy, and the kind of city you want to understand.`

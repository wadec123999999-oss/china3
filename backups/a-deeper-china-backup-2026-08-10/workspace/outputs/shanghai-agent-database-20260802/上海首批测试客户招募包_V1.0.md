# 上海首批测试客户招募包 V1.0

目标：招募 3–5 位计划在未来 7–30 天内到上海的英语独立旅行者，测试 `Route Direction` 与 `Human-checked City Roadbook` 的真实价值、耗时和价格接受度。

这不是“免费旅行体验招募”，也不要求客户公开宣传。客户得到的是路线判断和数字路书；你得到的是获许可的匿名反馈与数据库改进证据。

## 一、筛选条件

优先：

- 已确定或大致确定上海日期；
- 1–3 晚，单人/情侣优先；
- 首访或二访，但能明确说出想要/不想要什么；
- 英语沟通，愿意在旅行后接受 15 分钟反馈；
- 接受自行预订、不要求实时陪同。

暂不接：

- 要代订、陪同、包车、导游或体验打包；
- 行程不足一天却要求覆盖全城；
- 严重医疗、无障碍、过敏或儿童安全需求，且希望你提供保证；
- 想通过路书获得私密宗教、工作室、茶农或教练接触。

## 二、公开招募帖（英文）

### 版本 A：面向熟人/社群介绍

> I’m building A Deeper China, a new kind of planning service for independent travelers: not a tour, not a booking platform, and not a generic AI itinerary.
>
> I’m looking for 3–5 English-speaking travelers visiting Shanghai in the next month who would be open to testing a human-checked digital roadbook. It is designed around what you want to understand, what you want to avoid, your real arrival/departure times, and a practical fallback if the day changes.
>
> You will still make all reservations and payments directly. I’m testing the product, so I will ask for a short feedback conversation after your trip. If this sounds useful, send me your dates, how many real days you have, and one thing you do *not* want your Shanghai trip to become.

### 版本 B：适合小范围私信

> Hi [Name] — I’m testing a small, independent-travel roadbook product for Shanghai. It is for travelers who want more than a landmark list: a route built around their actual time, interests, and a realistic fallback.
>
> I remembered you may be visiting Shanghai around [date/context]. If you are still planning, would you be open to a short trip brief? No obligation, no booking service, and I would only ask for honest feedback if we make a roadbook together.

不要说：`free VIP itinerary`、`exclusive local access`、`guaranteed experience`、`last chance`。

## 三、首轮联系渠道与上限

| 渠道 | 做法 | 上限 |
|---|---|---:|
| 朋友/前同事/海外熟人 | 只联系确实可能近期来上海的人 | 10 位 |
| 已有网站/内容订阅者 | 一封透明测试邀请 | 1 次 |
| 有明确发帖规则的旅行社群 | 先问管理员，获允许才发 | 每群 1 帖 |
| 个人内容账号 | 发布“上海两天如何删减”的内容，CTA 是 brief，不是强招募 | 每周 1 次 |

不在 Reddit 评论区或他人视频下复制招募文案；先回答问题，且只在允许时透明提及产品。

## 四、客户确认信息（回复后发出）

> Thank you — before I can tell whether this is a fit, could you send:
>
> 1. Your arrival and departure times in Shanghai;
> 2. Where you are staying, or the area you are considering;
> 3. The two things you most want from the trip;
> 4. The two things you would rather avoid;
> 5. Whether walking, stairs, rain, food restrictions, children, or timing need special consideration.
>
> A Deeper China provides independent travel information only. You would book any transport, venues, food, or accommodation directly.

## 五、测试档报价方式

不要一开始用“免费”掩盖价值。推荐二选一测试，记录客户反应：

### 方案 1：真实付费验证

> The standard test price is US$59 for a Human-checked City Roadbook for one city, up to three nights, for one or two travelers. It includes a main route, a fallback, your direct action list, and one written revision. I am asking all early customers for a short feedback conversation after the trip.

### 方案 2：研究合作价（只给 1–2 人）

> I have two research-participant places for this first round. The roadbook is offered at [your chosen reduced price], in exchange for a 15-minute feedback call and permission to use anonymous product feedback. It is still an independent-travel roadbook, not a tour or booking service.

若客户明确不愿付费，不要立刻送完整定制路书。可给 `Route Direction`，记录拒绝原因。

## 六、测试客户欢迎页（路书首页前置）

> **Welcome to your A Deeper China test roadbook.**
>
> This is a human-checked digital planning document for independent travel. It is built around the trip brief you confirmed on [date]. It includes a suggested main line, an alternative when conditions change, and the details you still need to verify or book directly.
>
> It does not include tickets, transport, restaurants, accommodation, a guide, real-time support, medical advice, or any guarantee of weather, access, availability, or private experiences.
>
> Your feedback will help us improve the product. We will only quote feedback publicly with your separate permission, and will keep it anonymous unless you ask otherwise.

## 七、旅行后反馈邀约

> I hope Shanghai gave you some good surprises. Would you be open to a 15-minute feedback conversation this week? I am most interested in what the roadbook helped you decide, what became irrelevant once you arrived, and what a generic AI answer would have missed.
>
> You can also reply in three lines:
> - The most useful part was…
> - The part that did not match reality was…
> - I would / would not pay for this again because…

## 八、合格测试的最低证据

一次测试只有同时满足以下条件，才可作为产品学习样本：

- [ ] 客户完成并确认 `travel_brief`；
- [ ] 记录从招募来源到 brief 完成的转化；
- [ ] 有真实的人工审核留痕，或明确标为 research-led；
- [ ] 客户实际旅行或明确说明未成行；
- [ ] 收到反馈或至少两次合理的反馈邀请记录；
- [ ] 回写一次数据库/追问/路书的具体改动；
- [ ] 未将客户姓名、住址、支付/证件或健康细节写进公开材料。

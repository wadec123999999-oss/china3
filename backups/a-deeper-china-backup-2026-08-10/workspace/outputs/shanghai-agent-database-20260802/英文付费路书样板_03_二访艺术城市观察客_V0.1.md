# A Deeper China | Shanghai Beyond the Postcard

**For:** a repeat visitor interested in art, design and urban transformation  
**Pace:** moderate, programme-led, with room for one serious exhibition  
**Status:** editorial sample. Exhibition programming, venue access, route continuity and evening conditions must be checked before delivery.

## The idea of this trip

This is not an “undiscovered Shanghai” promise. It is a way to leave the postcard behind by asking how the city reuses infrastructure, curates art and builds public space.

## Day 1 — Choose art for a reason

Start with a current exhibition, but choose it only after we know your medium and interests. Museum of Art Pudong, a West Bund institution, M50 or another space is not automatically right because it appears on a list. The right choice is the one that gives you a useful encounter with contemporary practice, architecture or a collection you cannot see elsewhere.

Pair the exhibition with a short riverfront walk. Do not make the day a gallery-counting exercise. The conversation should be about what cultural institutions mean in a city whose skyline is constantly updated.

## Day 2 — Yangpu Riverside: work, infrastructure, public space

Yangpu is the better choice when you want urban systems, not another historic façade. Industrial structures along the river record a period when energy, water and manufacturing made this edge important. Today, the meaningful question is not “what was abandoned?” but “what was preserved, removed, reprogrammed or made publicly accessible?”

Use a short field-checked segment rather than a heroic full-length route. Add a museum only if its programme adds to the labour, skills or industrial story.

## Day 3 — Return to the central city with a sharper eye

Walk a carefully selected Bund / Rockbund / People’s Square thread. Having already seen the riverfront as industry and public space, you can now read the central city as a changing collection of institutions: finance, culture, commerce and municipal ambition.

End with a choice:

- **Stay in Shanghai** if you still want public-life, neighbourhood or food research.
- **Add Hangzhou/Suzhou** only if a different Jiangnan rhythm is the actual goal; an extension should change the question, not merely add another famous name.

## What this route excludes

- No generic café list.
- No automatic “hidden gems.”
- No specific gallery, restaurant or venue claim without current programme and access verification.
- No treating industrial or residential areas as sets for content creation.

# A Deeper China | Guangzhou, first visit (human-check draft)

**For:** A first-time visitor with two full days, interested in food, architecture and a city that feels different from Shanghai or Shenzhen.

## The idea

Guangzhou should not be consumed as a restaurant ranking with a colonial-photo stop. Use food to enter the city, then connect a trading-port layer, older urban fabric and a contemporary riverfront.

## Day 1 — Eat into Liwan

Begin with a Cantonese meal format that fits your dietary needs, group size and willingness to queue. Then use Chen Clan Ancestral Hall as a precise architectural anchor before a flexible Liwan / Shamian walk.

Shamian is one layer of Guangzhou's port history, not the whole story. Keep food expectations separate from the historical walk.

**Human check:** current access, weather, meal timing, mobility and any named reservation/ticket rule.

## Day 2 — The city across time

Choose one old-city public-space layer—arcades, religious/urban density and respectful street observation—or a contemporary Pearl River / Zhujiang New Town layer. The best choice depends on whether you want city texture, architecture, night photography or a lighter day.

Use a food format in the evening rather than forcing another landmark.

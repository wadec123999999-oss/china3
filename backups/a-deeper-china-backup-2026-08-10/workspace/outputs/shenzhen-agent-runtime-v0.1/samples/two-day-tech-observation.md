# A Deeper China | Shenzhen technology-observation draft

**Shape:** 2 days, built around What a Shenzhen tech trip actually is · Public-facing technology and city interfaces · Huaqiangbei: supply-chain observation, not a bargain hunt.

## Why this Shenzhen
This is not a fictional factory tour. It uses public evidence—hardware markets, consumer interfaces, design and urban systems—to make a technology city legible.

## Suggested building blocks
- **What a Shenzhen tech trip actually is** — What are you trying to understand: hardware, consumer tech, manufacturing, AI, EVs, city systems or start-up culture?
- **Public-facing technology and city interfaces** — Where can I see technology as part of daily urban life rather than a corporate visit?
- **Huaqiangbei: supply-chain observation, not a bargain hunt** — How do I explore an electronics market without being misled about prices, quality or sourcing access?

## On-the-ground notes
- **Technology intent interview:** 'Tech' can mean consumer gadgets, sourcing, manufacturing systems, mobility, AI, design or curiosity about modern China. A good Shenzhen plan starts by separating these needs. **Check:** vague_intent, expectation_mismatch.
- **One-day Shenzhen tech core:** A credible single-day structure is one hardware-market observation block plus one public innovation/design/city layer, with recovery time. **Check:** overpacking, heat, market_fatigue.
- **Public flagship/showroom decision:** Public retail or showrooms can help a visitor see how Chinese technology is presented to consumers, but are not factory or engineering access. **Check:** venue_turnover, access_rules, sales_pressure.
- **Metro, delivery and payment as urban interface:** For many visitors, the most legible technology is the public interface: transit, payments, maps and platform-mediated urban services. **Check:** payment, connectivity, app_setup, language.
- **Huaqiangbei observation walk:** A visible interface to components, repair, retail, accessories and the density of a hardware ecosystem; strongest for observation rather than purchasing. **Check:** quality_risk, counterfeit_risk, language, crowds, payment.
- **Huaqiangbei buying decision:** Buying a cheap accessory is different from sourcing parts, buying devices or trusting warranty claims. **Check:** warranty, compatibility, counterfeit_risk, export_rules, payment.

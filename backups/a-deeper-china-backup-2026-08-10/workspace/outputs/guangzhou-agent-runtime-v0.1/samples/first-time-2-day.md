# A Deeper China | Guangzhou draft

**Shape:** 2 days, built around Cantonese food as a social system · Liwan: old city, water and trade · Craft, clan and architectural language.

## Why this Guangzhou
This is not a restaurant ranking plus a colonial-photo stop. It connects eating, port-city layers, public old-city texture and one contemporary contrast.

## Suggested building blocks
- **Cantonese food as a social system** — How do I eat Guangzhou without receiving a stale restaurant list?
- **Liwan: old city, water and trade** — How do Shamian, Xiguan and Liwan fit together?
- **Craft, clan and architectural language** — What makes traditional Guangzhou visible?

## On-the-ground notes
- **Morning yum cha decision:** Dim sum is a time-based, shared meal format and an introduction to Cantonese social rhythm, not merely a list of dishes. **Check:** queues, dietary_needs, ordering_language, meal_timing.
- **Roast meat / noodle / snack decision:** A smaller food format can be a better match than a major dim-sum or banquet commitment, especially for solo visitors or dietary caution. **Check:** ingredients, allergens, translation, queues.
- **Shamian Island:** A compact entry to Guangzhou's trading-port and colonial-era built environment, best read as one historical layer rather than as a claim about the whole city. **Check:** weather, tourist_density, commercial_noise.
- **Liwan Lake–Xiguan walking fabric:** A slower way to connect water, older residential/commercial fabric and a food-oriented district. **Check:** heat, rain, walking_distance, venue_turnover.
- **Chen Clan Ancestral Hall:** A strong material lens on clan organisation, carving and architecture; it gives traditional Guangzhou a specific reading question. **Check:** ticketing, crowds, opening_time.
